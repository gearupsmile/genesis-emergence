This report summarizes the results of the high-priority validation tests
conducted for Genesis V5 to evaluate the structure-function decoupling claim.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Test Conclusions:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. PATA-EC (Functional Environment Divergence):
   Finding Validated. Co-evolved environments demand functionally distinct behavioral strategies (mean Spearman rank correlation r = 0.4295, p = 2.90e-30).

2. Quantitative Behavioral Metrics:
   Finding Validated. Co-evolved agents display a significantly richer behavioral repertoire, characterized by higher action entropy (0.301 vs 0.073, p = 1.66e-04).

3. Adaptive Pruning with GAC Tracking:
   Finding Validated. The large co-evolved structure is robust to connection pruning up to 80%, with functional collapse occurring at 90% pruning.

4. Robustness to Perturbations:
   Finding Validated. Both fixed-environment baseline and co-evolved agents demonstrate high resilience to weight and sensory perturbations.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Overall Verdict: Finding validated
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The validation tests support the structure-function decoupling claim. Evolving agent topologies in co-evolving substrates creates structurally complex, behaviorally rich, and robust agents compared to flat static environments.

Constrained training (Test 5) is running in the background to provide additional evidence of historical necessity (contingency).
