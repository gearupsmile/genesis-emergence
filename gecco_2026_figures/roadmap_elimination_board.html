Experimental Roadmap

Click through the steps of our three-experiment investigation

Step 1 — Experiment V2

Complete Fitness Removal

"Can constraint-driven selection sustain evolutionary activity without any fitness function?"
Finding: Yes — but it hits a hard ceiling. EPC plateaus at 140–155. Activity without open-endedness.
Step 2 — Experiment V3

Sham-Controlled Secretion

"Does letting agents write to their environment break the ceiling?"
Finding: No. 1,000,000 generations of secretion yields zero complexity growth. Real vs Sham identical (LZ76 = 0.068). Niche construction is causally insufficient without representation.
Step 3 — Experiments V4 & V5

CPPN Dev & Speciation

"What if we change the genome representation itself?"
Finding: Breakthrough. Neural nodes grow from 12 to 520. Co-evolution drives 4.2x more structural growth. Open-ended evolution unlocked.
